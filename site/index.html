Your movies,
on the big screen.

Beam plays the movies on your Mac on any Chromecast or Google Cast TV. Drop a file, press play.

Download for Mac View on GitHub
Free for personal use · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon & Intel

Everything a movie night needs

▶︎

Plays what you have

MP4, MKV and MOV up to 4K HEVC, streamed straight from your Mac with no upload or conversion.

Aa

Subtitles that just work

Finds subtitles next to the movie or inside it. SRT, VTT, ASS. Fix timing and style while you watch.

♫

Audio tracks

Switch language or commentary tracks, and Beam remembers your preference.

≡

Up Next

Drop a whole season. Episodes play back to back, with shuffle and repeat.

↺

Resume anywhere

Every movie picks up where you stopped, even days later.

⌨︎

Media keys

Play, pause and skip from your keyboard or Control Center while Beam stays in the background.

Install

  1. Download Beam.dmg (drag to Applications) or Beam.pkg (installer) from the latest release.
  2. Open Beam and allow Local Network access so it can find your TV.
  3. Optional: brew install ffmpeg for poster frames, embedded subtitles and audio track switching.
